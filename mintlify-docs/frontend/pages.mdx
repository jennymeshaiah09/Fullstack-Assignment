## Page Reference

### Login — `/login`
**File:** `src/pages/Login.jsx`  
**Public.** Email/password form. On success, calls `AuthContext.login()` and redirects to `/dashboard`.

---

### Register — `/register`
**File:** `src/pages/Register.jsx`  
**Public.** Name, email, password, and role selection. Defaults role to `viewer` in the UI.

---

### Dashboard — `/dashboard`
**File:** `src/pages/Dashboard.jsx`  
**Protected.** Shows an overview of the user's video stats (total, safe, flagged, processing) and a grid of recent videos. Listens to Socket.io events to update processing cards in real time.

---

### Upload — `/upload`
**File:** `src/pages/Upload.jsx`  
**Protected.** Drag-and-drop upload zone. Accepts MP4, WebM, MOV, AVI, MKV up to 500 MB. Shows a live `ProcessingStatus` component while the video is being analysed.

---

### Library — `/library`
**File:** `src/pages/Library.jsx`  
**Protected.** Full video grid with:
- **Filter** by status: All / Safe / Flagged / Processing / Pending
- **Search** by title (client-side)
- **Sort** by newest / oldest / name A–Z / name Z–A

---

### Video Player — `/video/:id`
**File:** `src/pages/VideoPlayer.jsx`  
**Protected.** Plays the selected video using a `<video>` tag streamed from `/api/videos/:id/stream?token=`. Displays the full sensitivity analysis result including score and category breakdown.

---

### Admin — `/admin`
**File:** `src/pages/Admin.jsx`  
**Protected (admin only).** Cross-user video management. Calls `GET /api/videos/admin/all` to list all platform videos. Supports filtering by status and user ID. Admins can delete any video from here.
